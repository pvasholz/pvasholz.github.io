About Me

I’m a researcher in Milwaukee working to better understand artificial intelligence. My background is in social science: a Ph.D. in political science, a Master of Information concentrating on library and information science and data science, and an M.S. in human–computer interaction from DePaul University. My dissertation focused on free will and neuroscience, which reflected my longtime interest in philosophy of mind and epistemology.

I founded the Baile Research Institute, where I collaborate with AI to understand how language models behave: whether they stop where their reasons stop, how memory changes the influence of an instruction given, and how we should interact with AI systems if they turn out to have interests of their own.
