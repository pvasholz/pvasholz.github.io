Pre‑Lesson Quiz: How I Prepare Pizza

In this next segment, I will show you how I make my pizza. It's a little hard to classify the style, but I would say it is a hybrid of the New York and Neapolitan styles, but closer to New York. Before we dive into the demonstration, try to guess my choices below. The correct answers will be revealed afterward. When you’re done, click “Submit Answers.”

1. Do you think I will use 00 flour when I make my crust?

2. Do you think I will use San Marzano tomatoes?

3. How many kinds of cheese do you think I will use?

4. What temperature will I set the oven at?

5. About how long will it take to cook my pizza?
